What is Fractional Investing?

Fractional investing lets you buy a portion of a stock instead of a whole share. This makes investing accessible to everyone — you can start with as little as ₹10.

💵

Low Entry Point

Invest any amount you're comfortable with. No need to buy a full share of expensive stocks like BAJFINANCE at ₹7,210.

📊

Diversify Easily

Spread your money across multiple stocks instead of putting all your eggs in one basket.

Reading a Stock Card

Here's what each element of a MicroVest stock card means:

Ticker Symbol The unique abbreviation for a stock (e.g., AAPL for Apple)
Current Price The latest simulated price — updates every 2 seconds
Change % How much the price changed since the last tick. Green = up, Red = down
Risk Badge Low / Medium / High — based on the stock's volatility
52-Week Range The lowest and highest price the stock has traded at in the past year
Invest Button Opens the calculator to buy fractional shares of the stock

How Our Simulator Works

MicroVest uses a simplified Geometric Brownian Motion model to simulate realistic stock price movements.

1

Base Prices

Each stock starts with a realistic base price drawn from recent market data.

2

Random Walks

Every 2 seconds, prices change based on each stock's volatility parameter, creating realistic-looking movements.

3

Volatility Controls Risk

Low-risk stocks (volatility: 0.005–0.008) move gently. High-risk stocks (0.018–0.025) swing dramatically.

4

Portfolio Impact

Your portfolio's value updates in real-time as prices change, showing realistic gains and losses.
